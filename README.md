# AI-Powered University Learning Platform

An AI-powered university learning platform designed to bring academic resources, students, educators, and intelligent learning assistance together in one centralized environment.

## Overview

University students often rely on fragmented resources such as lecture notes, previous exams, assignments, explanations, and study materials shared across different platforms and communities.

Our project aims to create a unified academic environment where students can discover, organize, and learn from university course resources while receiving personalized assistance throughout their learning journey.

The platform is built around a persistent academic knowledge base organized across universities, faculties, departments, and courses. Students can explore educational resources, contribute to the community, practice through generated assessments, and use intelligent learning tools to better understand their courses.

## Key Ideas

* **Centralized Academic Resources**
  Bring lectures, notes, previous exams, assignments, explanations, and other educational resources together.

* **AI-Powered Learning Assistance**
  Provide students with intelligent assistance based on their course materials and available academic resources.

* **Personalized Learning**
  Help students identify areas they need to improve and provide personalized study guidance.

* **Exam Preparation**
  Support students in preparing for exams through practice questions and customizable mock examinations.

* **AI Studio**
  Give students a workspace where they can work with selected learning resources, create notes, summaries, study guides, quizzes, and other study materials.

* **Community Learning**
  Allow students, teachers, and teaching assistants to share knowledge, participate in discussions, create learning paths, and curate useful collections of resources.

## Vision

Our vision is to go beyond the traditional Learning Management System and create a continuously growing academic environment where university knowledge can remain accessible across semesters and generations of students.

The ultimate goal is to help students **discover, understand, practice, and master** their university courses in a more personalized and connected learning experience.
